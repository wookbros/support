# support

Public issue tracker for WOOKBROS services.

## How to Use

1. Go to [Issues](https://github.com/wookbros/support/issues)
2. Click **New Issue**
3. Describe your question, bug report, or suggestion

Or email us at **wookbros.official@gmail.com**

## Categories

- 🐛 **Bug Report** — Something isn't working as expected
- 💡 **Feature Request** — Suggest a new feature or improvement
- ❓ **Question** — General questions about our services
